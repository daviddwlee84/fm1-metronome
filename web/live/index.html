The songs (as on the FM-1's white keys; a press here is one there)

In time

With the FM-1 clicking, press T (or the button) on 8 beats: the conductor and this page's clicks then land with its sound. Kept for USB and Bluetooth apart, in this browser.

The FM-1 tells this page only what plays and when, on a change and once a second; the page counts the beats on its own clock, so the link's jitter (USB a few ms, Bluetooth tens) does not reach the beat. What is left is a fixed delay, which the calibration takes out. From METRONOME 0.5 the page also times a round trip to the FM-1 each second and sets its clock by it, so the link's own delay (USB about 1 ms, Bluetooth tens) comes out too; what is left is the sound's way out of the FM-1 and of this computer.
