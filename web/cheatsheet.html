METRONOME cheatsheet

Firmware 0.3, every page and control. A stage metronome: 16 songs on the white keys. setlist editor · try it in the browser · source and install · print

How it is laid out

SET: the stagePick songs, see and change the tempo. Little else to touch, so little to get wrong mid-song.
HOME ⇄
The song's own pageEach song is one of two kinds (SEL switches): GRID, detailed: every step of the bar, beats, meter presets; CLASSIC, quick: four volumes and an accent, enough for practice without ever leaving it.

Settings save themselves 3 s after the last change. After a show: REC, hold SEL 2 s, PLAY clears the set for the next one.

Everywhere

PLAYstart / stop
HOMESET ⇄ the song's GRID or CLASSIC page
SELECTtempo ±1 (30–300); turned fast, ±5 a click
ALGOthe song's sound: CLICK, WOOD, BEEP (0.5: SET EDIT K3 may set one for all)
black keystap tempo (the last four taps); they flash with the beat. On SET while playing they do nothing (a stray touch on stage): tap in EDIT
MASTERvolume

The tempo's marking shows beside the BPM: LARGHISSIMO <40, LARGO 40, LARGHETTO 60, ADAGIO 66, ANDANTE 76, MODERATO 108, ALLEGRO 120, PRESTO 168, PRESTISSIMO 200 (the metronome's scale).

SET: the setlist

white keyplay that song; while playing it starts at the next bar (the key blinks until then)
OCT- OCT+tempo ±5
K1switch songs at the NEXT BAR or NOW
K2jump to the next tempo marking: 35, 50, 63, 70, 92, 112, 132, 184, 208
RECEDIT on / off

Stored songs' keys glow, the playing one is lit. The screen: tempo, marking, meter, a pendulum, and the 16 songs with their tempos.

SET: EDIT

white keypick it (an empty one starts as a copy of the song on); press it again on the beat to tap its tempo
SELECT, OCT±its tempo exactly
SEL + white keyclear that song (the last one stays)
SEL held 2 sCLEAR ALL? PLAY clears every song (one at 120, 4/4 stays); any other button, or 5 s, keeps them
K30.5: one sound for every song: OWN SOUND (each its own), CLICK, WOOD, BEEP (kept). The songs keep their own sounds, heard again at OWN SOUND; the volumes and accents stay theirs
K4Bluetooth on / off (kept; on by default). The header shows BT, grey until a computer connects, then blue. (0.5: EDIT's footer shows K3 and K4 in turn, 3 s each)
RECleave EDIT

GRID: detailed

white keya step of the bar: off → ghost → normal → accent. The key is as bright as it sounds
K1beats a bar, 1–9
K2a beat in quarters, 8ths, triplets, 16ths (16 steps at most)
K3meter presets: 2/4 3/4 4/4 5/4, 6/8, 6/8 IN 6, 9/8, 12/8, 5/8 3+2, 7/8 2+2+3
OCT±tempo ±5
RECtap tempo
SELthis song becomes CLASSIC

K1 and K2 reset the pattern (accent on 1, normal beats, ghosts between). In triplets the meter reads 6/8, 9/8, 12/8 and the BPM is the dotted quarter's (eighth = 120 is 40 here). 6/8 IN 6, 5/8 and 7/8 count the eighth.

CLASSIC: quick

K1–K4volume 0–7 of the quarter, eighth, triplet and sixteenth layers, mixed
OCT- OCT+BEAT: an accent every 1–9 beats, 0 none
any key, RECtap tempo
SELthis song becomes GRID

The rhythm-watch way. Lamps: red on the accent, green on the other beats.
