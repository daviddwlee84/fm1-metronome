Try METRONOME

METRONOME on an FM-1 in your browser: the same firmware you would install, run by the fm1-emu emulator. Press Start on the screen, then click the synth once so it hears your keyboard. Back to the setlist editor · cheatsheet · source.

Things to try

  1. Play. It starts on SET with one song at 120. PLAY: an accent and three beats; the pendulum swings, the black keys flash with the beat.
  2. A tempo. Turn SELECT (fast: 5 a click), or K2 to jump between ALLEGRO, PRESTO, ADAGIO … Or press REC, a white key, and tap it on the beat.
  3. A setlist. In EDIT (REC) press other white keys: each is a song with its own tempo. Out of EDIT, press one while playing: it starts at the next bar.
  4. A pattern. HOME: the song's GRID. Turn K3 to 6/8 or 7/8 2+2+3, click steps (keys) for accents and ghosts.
  5. Rhythm-watch style. On GRID press SEL: CLASSIC. K2 up adds eighths between the beats, OCT- sets the accent every 3.

Keys on the computer

  • The piano keys: A S D F … are white keys, W E T … the black ones.
  • Buttons and knobs: click or drag them on the panel.
  • The emulator's own guide has the whole map.

Here, not on an FM-1

  • The songs are kept in this browser, not on a device. To plan a real FM-1's setlist, use the setlist editor.
  • Timing follows your computer: a busy one makes the clicks less even than the real thing.
